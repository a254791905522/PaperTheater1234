Turn the dials.Match the shadow.

Paper Theater is a miniature stage of paper-cutout layers. Each layer has a dial that cycles through a small set of positions. Tap to advance a layer one step, watch how its shadow falls on the backdrop, and line everything up until the silhouette matches the target—using as few dial turns as you can.

Cycle each layer

Every paper layer owns a fixed list of states—depth, tilt, or placement. One tap on that layer’s dial moves it forward one notch. There is no free-form drag; the puzzle is choosing the right sequence of cycles.

Order matters

Layers stack in depth. A shape in front can hide or reveal part of the shadow from a layer behind it. The order you turn the dials can matter as much as the final positions you pick.

Four moves behind the curtain.

No clock counts down. You read the stage, advance dials, and compare the combined shadow to the target silhouette on the backdrop.

  3. Scene 03

    Advance one step

    Earn your stars
